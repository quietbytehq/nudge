# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- New table two, Paradise Bay: island artwork, volcano that swallows and erupts the ball,
  coconut and beach-ball bumpers, five surfboard targets, and a third flipper at the end
  of the pier (it moves with the left flipper). Own music and sounds.
- Table one is now called Cosmic Drift.
- Each table's name logo is shown on its table-select card and at the top of the play screen.

## 0.1.1
- The snap is published as `nudge-pinball` (the name `nudge` was already taken).

## 0.1.0
- First public build: arcade intro, table select, how-to-play screen, top ten.
- Table one, Deep Space: 8 bumpers, 14 targets, 21 lit posts, orbit lanes, spinner, black hole.
- Dynamic follow camera, full-table and reduced-motion views.
- Three graphics quality levels with automatic step-down on slower graphics chips.
