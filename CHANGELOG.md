# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- New table three, Alpenglow: a Swiss lakeside castle at dusk. Tower and wine-barrel bumpers,
  five heraldic shields, a castle whose gate holds the ball, a stone causeway ramp into a
  snowy peak that glows pink, iron lanterns and autumn trees. Own music and sounds.
- Alpenglow has its own rules: a shield stays lit once hit; lighting all five opens the
  castle gate for a 10,000 jackpot and a three-ball multiball; ramps made in a row pay more.
- The global top 100 gains an Alpenglow tab.
- All tables: comic hit words in star bursts (each table has its own set) and an animated
  letter-by-letter banner for awards and jackpots.
- Paradise Bay and Alpenglow: the third flipper sits higher up the table, and the corner
  covers are plain dark panels with a gold edge instead of busy artwork.

## 0.2.0
- New table two, Paradise Bay, built after its concept painting: beach-ball and coconut
  bumpers, a row of five standing surfboard targets, a tiki hut that holds and kicks the
  ball, tiki torches, scallop-shell playfield lights, palms and a volcano.
- The wooden pier is a ramp the ball rides: up the pier, through the volcano (which
  erupts) and down a chrome wire rail to the left inlane.
- A third flipper above the tiki hut, worked by the left flipper key.
- Near-side obstacles: a tiki-drum bumper below the hut, a harbour-buoy bumper at the foot
  of the jetty, a rock-islet bumper in the lagoon and two standing shell targets.
- Paradise Bay has its own music and sounds, and its own softer lighting.
- Global top 100: a top-ten score can also be posted to nudge.quietbytehq.uk (name, score and
  table only). The name screen says so, and Tab keeps the score on this computer instead.
- Table one is now called Cosmic Drift.
- New app icon.
- Each table's name logo is shown on its table-select card and on the play screen.

## 0.1.1
- The snap is published as `nudge-pinball` (the name `nudge` was already taken).

## 0.1.0
- First public build: arcade intro, table select, how-to-play screen, top ten.
- Table one, Deep Space: 8 bumpers, 14 targets, 21 lit posts, orbit lanes, spinner, black hole.
- Dynamic follow camera, full-table and reduced-motion views.
- Three graphics quality levels with automatic step-down on slower graphics chips.
