# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- New table two, Paradise Bay, built after its concept painting: beach-ball and coconut
  bumpers, a row of five standing surfboard targets, a tiki hut that holds and kicks the
  ball, tiki torches, scallop-shell playfield lights, palms and a volcano.
- The wooden pier is a ramp the ball rides: up the pier, through the volcano (which
  erupts) and down a chrome wire rail to the left inlane.
- A third flipper above the tiki hut, worked by the left flipper key.
- Paradise Bay has its own music and sounds, and its own softer lighting.
- Table one is now called Cosmic Drift.
- Each table's name logo is shown on its table-select card and on the play screen.

## 0.1.1
- The snap is published as `nudge-pinball` (the name `nudge` was already taken).

## 0.1.0
- First public build: arcade intro, table select, how-to-play screen, top ten.
- Table one, Deep Space: 8 bumpers, 14 targets, 21 lit posts, orbit lanes, spinner, black hole.
- Dynamic follow camera, full-table and reduced-motion views.
- Three graphics quality levels with automatic step-down on slower graphics chips.
